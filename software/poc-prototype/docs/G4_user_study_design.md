# G4 user study design (NOT RUN: needs real participants)

**Summary:** G4 (approved) asks whether at least 10 target users mostly prefer a hardware-specific affordance and state purchase intent at a stated price. No interviews have taken place and no results exist. Recruiting people or posting a screener is externally visible, so it needs Anand's go-ahead and wording approval.

## Participants

- 10-15 developers who pay per token (API key or cloud billing) and use an AI coding agent at least weekly, on Linux.
- Screener questions: which tool; billing type; monthly AI spend band; OS.

## Protocol (45 min, remote)

1. **Context (5 min):** how they track spend today and what they do about large contexts. Ask open questions only.
2. **Task A, software only (10 min):** run a scripted coding task with the TokenKey proxy plus a terminal stats view.
3. **Task B, "virtual device" (10 min):** the same task with a simulated OLED window (spend, savings, mode) and on-screen encoder/approve/reject buttons. P5 in the plan; not built yet, about 2 days of work.
4. **Paper/3D-print mock-up or photo** of the 50 × 40 mm device.
5. **Questions:**
   - Preference A vs B for each of approve, ambient display and mode knob, on a 5-point scale.
   - Van Westendorp price questions (too cheap / cheap / expensive / too expensive).
   - "Would you buy at $X?" with X fixed by Anand before the study.
   - A single follow-up: what would make them buy?
6. **Analysis:** G4 passes if more than half prefer B on at least one affordance **and** at least 30 % (proposed, to confirm with Anand) state purchase intent at the fixed price. Report raw counts. Don't extrapolate to a market size.

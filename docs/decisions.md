VDD_SOC total = C6-C10 (5 x 0.22uF) + C11 + C12 (2 x 4.7uF) + C13 (22uF) = 1.1 + 9.4 + 22 = 32.5uF nominal (about 33uF). HDG Table 2 lists 5 x 0.22uF + 1 x 4.7uF + 1 x 22uF; the second 4.7uF brings the total to the 33uF guidance. |

The 1.00 mm dimension refers to an unlabelled rectangle on the PCB back, protruding 1.00 mm, spanning y = 10.8 ... 13.8 mm from the header-side PCB edge, i.e. **+9.3 ... +12.3 mm from pin 1 (about 3.0 mm long in y)**, measured against the front view scale (glass top 4.87 and glass bottom 21.77 mm reproduce within 0.05 mm; side-view PCB is drawn about 1.8 mm short, so take +/-0.3 mm). **Its x position and x width are not shown anywhere on the drawing (unresolved).** Identity not stated: it is not the FPC bend (the FPC wraps from the glass ledge at y 21.8 mm to the PCB *front* at the bottom edge, pads 14.50 wide) and not the driver IC (SSD1315 is COG on the glass ledge, front, y 18.7-21.8 mm); most likely a back-side component (e.g. regulator/passive) or stiffener, unconfirmed. Also drawn: a thin (~0.1 mm) undimensioned outline on the back at y ~18.7-25.6 mm from the header edge, unidentified. Treat the whole back as up to 1.0 mm tall until a physical sample is measured. |

| OLED header height (issue #31) | 2026-10-19, TokenKey PM: **3.0 mm plastic header** (was 2.5 mm standard). Reason: the module's 1.0 mm back-side block sits at about y +9.3…+12.3 mm from J2 pin 1, x unknown. Per PM's rule an undetermined location means 3.0 mm, so the real gap under the module is 3.0 − 1.0 = 2.0 mm, which meets the 2.0 mm under-OLED height cap. **Block position unverified: measure a real module before going to a lower header.** The band y +9.3…+12.3 from pin 1 is where tall parts are riskiest. Mounting and stack height: see the issue #44 row (module soldered directly to the 3.0 mm header). J2 on the board moved to y 5.0 for ≥ 1.0 mm module-to-USB-C clearance (1.24 mm). |
| EC11 shaft (issue #45) | 2026-10-19, TokenKey PM: encoder stays **EC11E18244A5 with the 20 mm shaft**. The 15 mm swap recorded under issue #31 is **cancelled**, because shaft engagement into the knob would be too short. Same footprint, no board change. |
| USB-C cable exit and OLED orientation (issue #45) | 2026-10-19, TokenKey PM: the USB-C cable exits at the **rear** of the enclosure, and the OLED image is **rotated 180° in firmware** (`OLED_ROTATE_180`). |

| Enclosure weight (issue #53) | 2026-10-21, TokenKey PM (Anand's answer was still pending): keep the **slim 13.2 mm puck** and **drop the 120 g target**. Stability comes from five stock silicone feet (about 7.9 x 2.2 mm, in pockets) and a 1.2 mm steel plate under the board (about 49.8 x 39.8 mm, 11.7 g). The total is about 39.5 g. Interference check: 384 pairs, 0 clashes. |
